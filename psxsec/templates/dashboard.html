PSX sector risk and return

Which sectors of the Pakistan Stock Exchange paid best for the risk they carried, over the five years to October 2026. Equal-weighted sector indexes built from every company that trades regularly; dividends included.

Companies and sectors: Pakistan Stock Exchange data portal. Prices and dividends: Yahoo Finance. Safe rate: State Bank of Pakistan policy rate. Past performance does not predict future returns; this is analysis, not investment advice. Code, memo and method: github.com/Maira-W/psx-sector-risk-return. MIT licence.
